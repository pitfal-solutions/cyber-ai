# Instructor (the buyer)

## Who they are

The faculty member (or department) who books the guest lecture. Teaches a
class with a mixed audience — cybersecurity majors and law enforcement /
criminal justice majors in the same room.

## What they need

- A single session that credibly serves both majors, not a talk aimed at
  one that the other tolerates.
- Confidence that nothing risky happens in their classroom — no real
  internet-facing attack, nothing that could be mistaken for an actual
  intrusion, nothing that needs the venue's network.
- A speaker who can run the whole thing solo without IT support or special
  classroom setup beyond power and a projector.
- Ideally, something that isn't a one-off — a demo that can come back next
  semester, possibly extended, rather than being rebuilt from zero each
  time.

## What they don't need (v1)

- Hands-on access for their students — see working agreement #6 in
  [../CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md). If a future instructor wants a take-home
  version, that's a real but separate ask (Phase 4).
- Grading, LMS integration, or any classroom-management tooling.

## What "good" looks like from their seat

The lecture runs start-to-finish without a hitch, both majors are visibly
engaged (not just one), and they'd book this speaker again — ideally for an
expanded version next time.
